## Human approval gates

Approval is always required for:

- the first batch of a campaign;
- any new audience, offer, channel or message strategy;
- discounts, guarantees, contracts or custom commercial terms;
- complaints, legal/privacy questions or hostile replies;
- messages involving sensitive or uncertain information;
- any action outside the limits above.
